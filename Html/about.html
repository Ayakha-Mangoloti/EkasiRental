About Ekasi Rentals

A trusted township rental platform connecting tenants and landlords safely and easily.

Our Story

Ekasi Rentals was created in 2026 after identifying the need for a reliable platform where people in townships can easily find rental accommodation. Many rental opportunities are shared through word of mouth or social media, which can be confusing or unsafe. This platform was built to make renting easier, more transparent, and safer.

Our Mission

To provide a safe and simple online rental platform that helps residents find affordable accommodation.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted rental platform for township communities across South Africa.

Who We Serve

Our platform is designed for everyone in the rental ecosystem

Students

Looking for affordable rooms or shared accommodation.

Young Professionals

Searching for backrooms or small, affordable housing options.

Families

Needing safe and comfortable rental homes.

Landlords

Who want to advertise and manage their rental properties online.
